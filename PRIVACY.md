# Dipole privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Dipole is a board game made by David Aguiar González. This policy explains what the app does with
your information.

## Information collected

Dipole itself collects nothing. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal
or usage data, and it has no accounts of its own, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting
services. Achievements are the only thing that leaves your device, and only to your own Google Play
Games or Game Center account (see below). On Android the app has internet access for that and for
the store's review popup; it asks for no other permission.

## Achievements

On Android, if you use Google Play Games, the achievements you earn and your progress towards them
are saved to your Play Games profile by Google. On iOS, if you are signed in to Game Center, they
are saved to your Game Center account by Apple. Dipole only tells these services which achievement
you earned; it does not receive your name, email address or any other details from them.
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) apply to that data.

You can play without them: if you don't sign in to Play Games, or turn off Game Center in your
device's Settings, nothing leaves your device. You can delete your Play Games data from your Google
account, and your Game Center data from your Apple account.

## Ratings

From time to time, after a win, the app may ask Google Play or the App Store to show their own
review popup, and the menu can open Dipole's store page. Anything you write there goes to Google or
Apple under their policies; Dipole doesn't see it.

## Information kept on your device

The app keeps your settings (sound, appearance, last difficulty and side, whether you have seen the
tutorial), the games you choose to save, a few counters (games finished, wins against the computer,
app launches, and when it last asked for a review) and the achievements earned on this device. They stay on your device, in the app's private
storage, and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## Links

The rules screen links to Mark Steere's website. Opening a link takes you to your browser, and that
site's own privacy policy applies there.

## Children

Dipole itself collects no data from anyone, including children. Play Games and Game Center apply
their own rules for children's accounts.
