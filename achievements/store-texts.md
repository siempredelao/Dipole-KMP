# Achievement texts for the stores

Paste these into Play Console (Play Games Services → Achievements) and App Store Connect (Game Center → Achievements). The stores show them, not the app.

| Key | Play name (`achievement_<key>` in games-ids.xml) | Apple ID | Type | Suggested points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `first_steps` | First steps | `gc.david.dipole.first_steps` | Standard | 5 |
| `first_victory` | First victory | `gc.david.dipole.first_victory` | Standard | 5 |
| `friendly_match` | Friendly match | `gc.david.dipole.friendly_match` | Standard | 5 |
| `rising_player` | Rising player | `gc.david.dipole.rising_player` | Standard | 20 |
| `grandmaster` | Grandmaster | `gc.david.dipole.grandmaster` | Standard | 50 |
| `both_sides` | Both sides | `gc.david.dipole.both_sides` | Standard | 20 |
| `flawless` | Flawless | `gc.david.dipole.flawless` | Standard | 30 |
| `no_help_needed` | No help needed | `gc.david.dipole.no_help_needed` | Standard | 50 |
| `big_capture` | Big capture | `gc.david.dipole.big_capture` | Standard | 20 |
| `swift_victory` | Swift victory | `gc.david.dipole.swift_victory` | Standard | 20 |
| `regular` | Regular | `gc.david.dipole.regular` | Incremental, 10 steps | 30 |
| `dipole_master` | Dipole master | `gc.david.dipole.dipole_master` | Incremental, 50 steps | 100 |
| `ai_impostor` | AI impostor | `gc.david.dipole.ai_impostor` | Standard | 50 |

Name the English achievements exactly as above: Play turns the name into the resource name ("AI impostor" → `achievement_ai_impostor`), which the app looks up. All are revealed (none hidden). On Apple, use the description for both the pre-earned and earned text, and points out of Apple's 1000.
