# Dipole privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Dipole is a board game made by David Aguiar González. This policy explains what the app does with
your information.

## Information collected

None. Dipole does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data.
It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not ask for
internet access or any other permission.

## Information kept on your device

The app keeps your settings (sound, appearance, last difficulty and side, whether you have seen the
tutorial) and the games you choose to save. They stay on your device, in the app's private
storage, and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## Links

The rules screen links to Mark Steere's website. Opening a link takes you to your browser, and that
site's own privacy policy applies there.

## Children

Dipole collects no data from anyone, including children.
